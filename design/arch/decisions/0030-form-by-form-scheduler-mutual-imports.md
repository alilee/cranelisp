# 0030 — Form-by-form scheduler deadlocks on mutual imports; `super` safe for non-mutual patterns

The v4 form-by-form scheduler (`pipeline-v4.md` §3, `concurrent-pipeline.md` §7) has an architectural constraint: two modules A and B that each import from the other will deadlock. Concretely, when A's Pass-0 (signature registration / form classification) encounters `(import [B [*]])`, A moves to `TypecheckBlocked` waiting on B's symbols; when B's Pass-0 encounters `(import [A [*]])`, B moves to `TypecheckBlocked` waiting on A's. Neither module can make forward progress because each needs the other's signature set to resolve its own imports, and signatures are registered incrementally form-by-form rather than in a separate pre-pass. The `concurrent-pipeline.md` §7.1 item 1 assertion ("circular imports are rejected during dependency resolution") assumes a detector that catches the cycle before scheduling begins; that detector is out of scope for Ring 4 and the constraint is the operative reality. **Unsafe patterns**: (a) parent module P that imports from child C while C imports from P — including the idiomatic case where C uses `(import [super [*]])` (frontend-rewritten to `(import [P [*]])` per `super-import-arbitration.md`) and P imports from C; (b) any two top-level sibling modules that import from each other. The issue is not specific to `super` — `super` is merely the shorthand that makes the child-imports-from-parent pattern ergonomic, so it is the most likely trigger in practice. **Safe patterns**: (a) one-directional imports (the common case — most code); (b) a child using `(import [super [*]])` when the parent does NOT import from that child (common for utility-accessor submodules); (c) **test submodules using `discover-tests` + `run-test` builtins** to find and invoke sibling/parent test functions without needing `(import [super [*]])` at all — this is the canonical pattern for test scaffolding and is the recommended way to avoid the constraint when writing tests. **Future work**: a module-system redesign — separating dependency resolution for signatures from bodies, or adopting a different scheduling strategy — could lift the constraint. This is flagged as future research for a dedicated module-system sprint; it is NOT on the roadmap as planned work. Canonical location: `src/scheduler.rs` (worker loop) and `src/worker.rs` (form-by-form classification and `block_for_typecheck` invocation). See `super-import-arbitration.md` for the semantics discussion and `concurrent-pipeline.md` §7.1 for the scheduler-level properties the constraint sharpens. Rationale: Principle 6 (complexity budget — fixing this requires a major scheduler redesign; the workaround is clear, cheap, and ergonomic given `discover-tests`) + Principle 8 (no interim implementation — we do not half-fix with a timeout-based deadlock detector or a speculative one-shot retry; the constraint is either permanent or lifted by a real redesign, not papered over).
