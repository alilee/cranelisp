## Phase 6 — lenient-eval demonstration (user-directed)

User asked (Phase 6 framing): can a map-reduce over a `Vec` be written in terms of `let` so it parallelises under lenient eval? **Empirically proven YES (~3.6× wall-clock)** via index-range divide-and-conquer: the two recursive halves are independent sparkable `let` bindings; the split-point is inlined (binding `mid` first defeats sparking — confirmed negative control ≈1×); the Vec is a function param (not a same-block binding) so leaf `vec-get` doesn't interfere. Negative controls (prior-`mid`, linear fold) ≈1×. Phase 6b builds this as a committed test (positive + negative) + a teaching example. `/repl` demo optional.
