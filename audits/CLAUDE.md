# audits/

Temporary whole-context assessments, owned by `audit`. The
[audit contract](../.agents/skills/audit/SKILL.md) governs assessment; the
[rolling-audit method](../sprints/METHOD.md#27-rolling-whole-context-audit)
governs rotation and disposition.

- Keep an assessment here while its findings await extraction and disposition.
- Preserve every unaddressed point in its canonical action, existing filing or
  owning standing document. Reuse an existing carrier rather than duplicating it.
- Keep undecided recommendations pending disposition; recording them does not
  approve implementation, accept a residual or resolve a defect.
- Retire historical reports and their diagrams to Git once that extraction is
  complete. Cite their original checkpoint when historical evidence is needed.
- An audit report is dated evidence, not current design or requirement authority.

This memory establishes the collection below. References remain checked; no
historical exemption.

| Collection | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `audit-assessments` | Temporary audit assessments awaiting extraction and disposition. |
